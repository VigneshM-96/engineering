An bra-ket notation is a highly tech labled system.

1) ket -  where the arrow pointing |0>
2) superposition -
    1) normal computer - either |0> or |1>
    2) quantum computer - |w> = |0> + |1>
3) bra - the mirror (shadow) <|
4) bracket testing
    1) <0|0> = 1 (perfect matching)
    2) <0|1> = 0  (no match)
